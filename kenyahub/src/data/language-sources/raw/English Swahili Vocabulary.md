## [Page 9 — Advertisement]

### ADVERTISEMENT.

—•♦•—

THIS Vocabulary was begun as an Index to 'Swahili Exercises' (by Bishop Steere), and can be used for that purpose. It assumed its present form by the addition of a large number of words from his collections in the 'Handbook of the Swahili Language,' and of a few from other sources.

As it is intended to be useful, not only to Europeans learning Swahili, but to Swahilis and others learning to read an English book, a description of the abbreviations is added from Bishop Steere's 'Grammar in Swahili.'
